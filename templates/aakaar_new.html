Asia's Largest Departmental College Festival

Aakaar is the annual festival of the Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Bombay. Since its inception in 2009, it has grown into Asia's largest departmental college festival — a platform where aspiring engineers innovate, create and learn.

From the International Civil Engineering Symposium to Bridge-IT, ConquerIT, CENEX, Research Debate and City Planning, Aakaar brings together competitions, lectures, workshops and lab visits that connect textbook mechanics with real engineering practice.
