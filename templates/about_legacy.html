Asia's Largest Departmental College Festival

Aakaar is the annual festival of the Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Bombay. Since its inception in 2009, it has become Asia's largest departmental college festival, offering a platform for aspiring engineers to innovate, create and learn.

Organised by the Civil Engineering Association (CEA), Aakaar acts as the link between soil mechanics, finite element analysis, smart mobility modelling and frontline infrastructure practice — pairing competitions and research with lectures, workshops and hands-on lab access.
