MSE WALL

Prize Worth : INR 10000

Registration Fee : INR 200

Venue : Civil Dept Parking Lot, IIT Bombay

Date : 19th March

Time : 9AM - 4PM

*Problem statement will be provided after registration

The MSE Wall competition entails the conception and implementation of a prototype for a mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) retaining wall, intended to serve as a transitory partition through the utilization of reinforced paper and adhered to a paper-facing wall

This competition is happening live, and teams are required to participate at IIT Bombay on March 19th. Entry will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis

  • Registration: On-spot registration will be conducted.
  • Live competition : 19th March
  • Prizes worth INR 10,000 will be awarded to top 3 winners.
  • Aakaar IIT Bombay will present certificates of participation to all qualified teams.
  • Winners will be featured on our social media accounts/magazine and their testimonials will be featured on Aakaar’s official website.
  • This competition is a live competition.
  • Team has to build the MSE wall according to the specification given in Problem Statement.
  • Participate in a team of 4 members.
  • Participants from different colleges/organizations can form a team.
  • Each team member should be a UG/PG/student of some recognized college or schools irrespective of specialization.
  • Aakaar IIT Bombay reserves the right to disqualify any team if they do not follow above-mentioned instructions.
  • Aakaar, IIT BOMBAY reserves the right to change any or all of the above rules as they deem fit. Change in rules, if any, will be highlighted on the website and notified to the registered participants.
