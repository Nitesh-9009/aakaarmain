LogiQ will be a three-phase quiz series with each release in November, December, and January

  • LogiQ is an online quiz series for civil engineering enthusiasts.
  • You will have the chance to learn, explore, and showcase your skills and abilities in civil engineering.
  • The competition deals with topics varying from basic Civil Engineering concepts to advanced engineering ideas in a way that undergraduates from any year of study can participate in the competition.
  • We have launched this competition for those who don’t call for a lucid structure of questions to answer them. Prepare yourself to scratch your head while solving some of the most amorphous questions of your life.

  • LogiQ will be a three-phase quiz series with each release in November, December, and January.
  • LogiQ consists of Multiple Choice Questions, True or False, Fill in blanks, and Match the Followings type of questions.
  • Each answer to the given question is unique.
  • All participants have to solve the questions in a specified time interval.
  • Phase 1: 5th November, 11AM - 11PM
  • Phase 2: 9th December, 11AM - 11PM
  • Phase 3: 7th January, 11AM - 11PM
  • Prizes worth INR 20,000 will be awarded to the top five winners based on the best score of the Overall Quiz Series.
  • The top 10 winners of the Overall Quiz Series will get a chance to attend workshops, lectures, and exhibitions in the main event at discounted rates.
  • The Top 10 participants of the monthly puzzle will be featured on our social media accounts and receive a certificate of excellence.
  • You can register for this competition individually only.
  • Undergraduate, Post graduate and PhD students from any college with any civil engineering background can participate in this competition.
  • We will consider the timestamp of the submission in case of a tie.
  • Throughout the series, your email address will serve as your unique ID.
  • Plagiarism that involves social media or other means will immediately disqualify you.
  • Team Aakaar, IIT Bombay, has the final decision-making authority.
