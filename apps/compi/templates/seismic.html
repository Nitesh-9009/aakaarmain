Abstract description:

An abstract is a concise version of your model focusing on the essentials of a more significant idea. It should be sufficient for reviewers to recognize the nature and significance of your model and the fairness of your approach.

The following elements must be included in the abstract:

  • Team Name
  • Names of all the team members.
  • Specific details of the earthquake-resistant approach to be used in the model's construction.
  • Photos of the model-making process from the beginning to the present state of the model.
Note: Abstract should be in .pdf format
