A Platform to Showcase and Enhance Your Skills

Aakaar IIT Bombay is the annual technical festival of the Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Bombay. Since its inception in 2009, it has acted as an excellent platform for students across the country, giving them opportunities to showcase and enhance their skills at the highest level.

Aakaar is Asia's largest Civil Engineering festival. Many exciting competitions are held here, such as the International Civil Engineering Symposium, Bridge-IT, ConquerIT, the Civil Engineering National Exhibition, letting students compete and enhance their practical skills and knowledge. As part of a knowledge-sharing endeavour, software workshops are conducted in ETABS, Autodesk Civil 3D, STAAD Pro, BIM and Revit.

How to Compete at AAKAAR

01

Register

Sign up through the Aakaar registration portal and pick your competition. Cross-college teams are welcome across the tracks.

02

Submit & Qualify

Submit your report, design or model as per your competition's rulebook. Shortlists are announced ahead of the finals.

03

IIT Bombay Finals

Shortlisted finalists compete live during the festival at the IIT Bombay campus in Powai.
