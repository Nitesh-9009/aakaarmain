About BridgeIt

  • BridgeIT is a structural engineering competition organized under Aakaar, IIT Bombay, focusing on the design and construction of a double-decker, arch-supported bridge.
  • Participants are required to construct a lightweight bridge model using popsicle sticks, cotton threads, and Fevicol, strictly adhering to the prescribed material rules.
  • The bridge design must satisfy specified geometric and structural constraints to ensure maximum efficiency, stability, and structural integrity.
  • Completed bridges will undergo physical load testing, and performance will be evaluated based on load-to-weight ratio and dimensional accuracy.

Rules & Regulations

  • This is a bring-from-home competition; participants must arrive with their completed bridge models.
  • Certificates will be awarded to all teams that get shortlisted for the final event at IIT Bombay.
  • Team size may range from 1 to 4 members, open to all students from any recognized college.
  • Any form of misconduct, unsafe practices, or interference during inspection or load testing will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • The decision of the organizers regarding testing procedures, scoring criteria, penalties, and disqualification shall be final and binding.
