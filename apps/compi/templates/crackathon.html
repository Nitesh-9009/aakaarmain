About Crackathon

  • The competition spans from core engineering fundamentals to advanced analytical problem statements.
  • It is open to students from all academic years, ensuring inclusivity across experience levels.
  • The problems are application-oriented and inspired by real-world engineering scenarios.
  • The focus is on evaluating depth of conceptual understanding rather than correctness alone.
  • Participants are challenged to design, analyze, and optimize machine learning and computer vision models.

Key Incentives

Prizes

Cash prizes worth INR 20,000 awarded to the top 5 performers in the overall series.

Certificates

Participation and merit certificates for top-performing participants.

Resume Value

Strong resume point highlighting real-world problem solving in ML and engineering.

Rules & Regulations

  • Open to Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and PhD students from any college.
  • Tie-breaker: Submission timestamp.
  • Team Aakaar, IIT Bombay, has the final decision-making authority.
  • Dataset Usage: Participants must use only the provided dataset for training. No external datasets are allowed.
  • Pre-trained Models: Pre-trained models are allowed and encouraged.
  • Team Size: Teams are limited to a maximum of 4 members.
  • Final Decision: The final ranking will be based on the mAP score calculated on the private test set. The organizers' decision is final.
