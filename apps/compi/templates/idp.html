About IDP

  • IDP serves as a professional conduit, dedicated to closing the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application in civil engineering.
  • Participants engage in real-world challenges presented by industry leaders, aligning academic insights with industry needs.
  • Contestants employ a strategic blend of innovation, exhaustive research, and creative acumen to formulate sophisticated solutions.
  • Offers a unique platform for addressing substantive challenges within the civil engineering domain set by industry trailblazers.
  • Open to all Students.

Structure & Submission

Abstract Guidelines

An abstract is a concise version of your model focusing on essentials. It must include:

  • Team Name & Names of all members.
  • Specific details of the approach to be used.
  • Photos of the model-making process (if applicable).
  • Format: .pdf file
Submission Process
  1. Add your Abstract file (.pdf) to your Google Drive.
  2. Rename the folder as <Team name>_<Aakaar_IDP>.
  3. Ensure the folder has "Anyone with the link" access.
  4. Submit the link in the registration form.
Rounds
  • Round 1 (Abstract): Submit a PPT/PDF containing your work on the problem statement.
  • Round 2 (Final): Shortlisted participants will present their solution to judges during the main fest at IIT Bombay.
