International Civil Engineering Symposium

Aakaar IIT Bombay is proud to launch its Eleventh International Civil Engineering Symposium (ICES) to encourage young research enthusiasts and act as a platform to exchange ideas and scientific knowledge.

Collaborative Research, Grown Here

We believe the Symposium will lead to the fruitful germination of collaborative research work. We look forward to bringing together and supporting talented and enthusiastic people who are inclined toward discovering, learning and innovating new domains in civil engineering.

The Eleventh International Civil Engineering Symposium invites young researchers to present, discuss and defend their work in front of experienced professors and industry leaders — and to build a network that carries beyond the festival.
