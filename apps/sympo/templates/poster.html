About Poster Presentation

The Technical Poster Presentation is a platform to communicate your research and understanding of a topic in a concise, visually capturing format. It challenges participants to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize ideas creatively.

Our Motive
  • Encourage, promote, and support Research in Civil Engineering.
  • Bring together Academia, Industry Professionals, and Research Scholars.
  • Provide an excellent platform for exchanging new ideas and cross-collaboration.

"Analyze, evaluate, and creatively demonstrate your research findings."

Rules & Specs

  • Team Size: Register in a team of 1-3 members.
  • Poster Size: A0 Portrait orientation.
  • Material: Thickness of 180-250 GSM.
  • Display: Must stand free on a supporting table (using hinged joints or back supports).
  • Judging: Based on Originality, Significance, and Presentation.

Abstract Topics

Technical Posters are invited in the following fields:

  • Concrete Technology & Building Materials
  • Geotechnical Engineering
  • Hydraulic & Water Resources / Ocean Engg
  • Remote Sensing (GPS/GIS)
  • Structural Engineering
  • Transportation Engineering
  • Environmental Engineering
  • Architecture, Infrastructure & Urban Science
