About Us

A Variety of Innovative and Exciting Events

Aakaar IIT Bombay is the annual technical festival of the Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Bombay. With a variety of innovative and exciting events, we uphold the legacy of conducting inter-college civil events with the best ideas and creativity from every civil enthusiast from the best institutions of the nation — adding more creative and mind-tinkering events this year.

Events like Lectures, Workshops, the Civil Engineering National Exhibition (CENEX), the Civil Expo and many more fun events are a part of Aakaar, making learning fun. It instigates students to innovate, create and learn through exciting events and competitions.
