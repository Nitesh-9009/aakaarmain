EVENTS

Aakaar IIT Bombay, the annual technical festival of the Department of Civil Engineering, has been a beacon of excellence since its inception in 2009.
It stands as Asia's largest Civil Engineering festival, providing a stellar platform for students nationwide to showcase and elevate their skills to the highest level.

ABOUT US

Aakaar IIT Bombay is the annual technical festival of the Department of Civil Engineering IIT Bombay. With a variety of innovative and exciting events, we uphold the legacy of conducting inter college civil events with the best ideas and creativity from every Civil enthusiast from best institutions of the nation adding more creative and mind tinkering events this year. Events like Lectures, Workshops, Civil Engineering National Exhibition(CENEX), Civil Expo and many more fun events are a part of Aakaar, making learning fun. In its 15th edition, it is all set to scale new heights, with many recent events and competitions providing an excellent platform for aspiring civil engineers to explore and learn about the stream's vastness. It instigates students to innovate, create and learn through exciting events and competitions.
